Support the Pantry Project

Help support local food pantry efforts while keeping The Happy Pantry free to use.

The Pantry Project is a mission of The Happy Pantry focused on helping local food banks across New Hampshire and Vermont. Each month, we choose a specific community and run a fundraising campaign to purchase food and supplies directly for a local pantry.

A simple way to support a local mission

Community support helps keep The Happy Pantry free to use while allowing us to provide simple meal planning tools, recipes, and up-to-date nutrition information that is easy to find, practical, and accessible for everyone.

How support is used

100% of donations support The Pantry Project. About 90% of funds raised go toward food purchases and supplies for local food banks, while the remainder helps cover administrative costs and the transportation and coordination needed to purchase and deliver food through the project.

The Pantry Project is operated through The Happy Pantry LLC by a small group of people passionate about improving access to food and nutrition. Keeping the project simple allows more of the funds raised to go toward food purchases and supplies for local pantries without the added administrative cost and complexity of maintaining a formal nonprofit organization.
