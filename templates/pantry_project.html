The Pantry Project

A giving mission powered by The Happy Pantry community.

Every month, our members can choose to donate directly to a cause that delivers the most needed — and least donated — foods to local pantries.

Filling the Gaps Left Behind

Canned goods are helpful, but they’re not enough. Fresh produce, meats, high-protein options, and culturally inclusive staples are what families actually need — but rarely receive.

The Pantry Project sources these exact items and partners directly with food pantries to distribute them where they’ll make the most impact.

How It Works

Step 1

When you sign up, you can optionally add $1, $3, $5, or more to your monthly subscription.

Step 2

We use 100% of these donations to source essential foods — not snacks, but staples like fresh produce and proteins.

Step 3

Each month, we deliver these items directly to community food pantries across the country.

See the Difference You Make

Every month, we’ll share reports on where donations went — what foods were delivered, which pantries were served, and how your support created real change.

Want to Help?

The Pantry Project is exclusive to Happy Pantry members. When you subscribe, you’ll have the option to give monthly.
